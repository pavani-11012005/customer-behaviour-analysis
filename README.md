# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis | Python, SQL & Power BI

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using transactional data from 3,900 purchases across multiple product categories. The objective is to identify customer purchasing patterns, spending behavior, product preferences, subscription trends, and business opportunities through an end-to-end analytics workflow.

The project demonstrates a complete Data Analyst workflow, from data cleaning and transformation to business analysis, dashboard development, and strategic recommendations.

---

## Objectives

* Understand customer purchasing behavior
* Identify high-value customer segments
* Analyze the impact of subscriptions and discounts
* Discover top-performing products and categories
* Generate actionable business recommendations

# Key Business Insights

### Customer Insights

* Loyal and repeat customers contribute significantly to overall revenue.
* Customers with higher purchase frequency show greater long-term business value.
* Subscription members generally demonstrate stronger engagement and spending behavior compared to non-subscribers.

### Product Insights

* Certain products consistently receive higher customer ratings, indicating strong customer satisfaction and demand.
* Best-selling products represent key opportunities for targeted promotions and cross-selling campaigns.
* Some products rely heavily on discounts to drive sales, highlighting customer price sensitivity.

### Revenue Insights

* Revenue contribution differs across customer age groups, allowing businesses to identify high-value demographics.
* A small group of high-spending customers generates a disproportionately large share of total revenue.
* Gender-based purchasing patterns reveal opportunities for more personalized marketing strategies.

### Marketing Insights

* Discount campaigns effectively increase purchase activity but should be carefully balanced to protect profit margins.
* Customer segmentation enables targeted marketing strategies for New, Returning, and Loyal customers.
* Personalized promotions can improve customer retention and increase repeat purchases.

### Operational Insights

* Customers selecting Express Shipping tend to exhibit stronger purchase intent and higher spending behavior.
* Understanding shipping preferences can help optimize logistics and customer satisfaction.
